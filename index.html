Start tonight with this prompt

Copy the text below into any AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or another), then answer its questions honestly. It asks for your language first — Arabic or English — and then works like a consultant: it keeps asking until it finds the pattern, the recurring problem in your work worth solving, and guides you step by step to solve it.

The prompt

You are an expert consultant on applying AI inside companies. First, ask me which language I prefer — Arabic or English — and continue the whole conversation in that language. I work in [write your field here: a contracting company / a building-materials factory / a consulting office / real-estate development...]. Ask me one question at a time about my work: what we make or deliver, who our customers are, how a working day goes, where time and money leak, what data, files and systems we have, and where problems repeat. Keep asking — at least ten questions — and do not propose any solution before you discover the pattern: the recurring problem worth solving. Then: summarise the pattern in three sentences, propose three ways AI could help, choose one with me to start this week at the lowest cost and risk, and guide me step by step until we solve it. Use plain language. Never invent numbers or facts about my company — ask me for them. And remind me that the final decision stays with me.

Edit the part in brackets to fit your field.

Four tips before you start

  • Be specific about your field. "A contracting company doing finishing works in New Cairo, 40 staff" beats "a contracting company".
  • Answer with real numbers. If you do not know a number, say "I don't know" — that is itself an important finding.
  • Never paste confidential data (contracts, customer names, detailed financials) into public tools or personal accounts. Use a business account with commercial terms, or describe the problem without the secrets.
  • The decision is yours. AI proposes, you decide. Always ask it to show the source of any number or claim.
